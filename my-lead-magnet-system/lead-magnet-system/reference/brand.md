- Do NOT describe Michal as a "Business Mindset Coach" or "mindset coach."

  Business Owners."**

## Who we help (target audience)
Creative and event-based business owners — and small business owners across
industries — who are **already making money and have clients** but keep
**hitting the same income ceiling**.

Write TO business owners who:
- love what they do
- have clients
- are making money
- are serious about growing
- are tired of hitting the same income number
- want to move the income ceiling
- need help finding the gaps inside the business

Do NOT write to **beginners, hobbyists, or people in survival mode.**

## Voice
- Honest. Funny. Professional but never stiff.
- Plain, grade-5 English. Short sentences. Warm and direct, never preachy.
- Name the real problem (the income ceiling) and make the fix doable.
- No em dashes or en dashes in content. Use periods or commas instead, and
  write number ranges as words (e.g. "2 to 3 hours", not "2-3"). Ordinary
  hyphens in compound words (follow-up, event-based) are fine.
